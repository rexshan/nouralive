This Policy explains how we handle personal data when you use BIBO or contact us about the app. It also explains your choices and how to request deletion of your account. For the rules governing use of the app, see the BIBO Terms of Service.

1. Personal data we process and why

Account and sign-in information
For phone sign-in, we use your mobile number, including its country calling code, and verification information to authenticate you and link the sign-in to your BIBO account. A phone number is required if you choose this sign-in method. If you sign in with your BIBO ID and password, we use those credentials to authenticate you.
Profile information
We use the profile details you enter, such as your display name and any country you select, to create and update your profile. If you choose to upload an avatar, we receive the image you select and use it as your profile photo. Uploading an avatar is optional. Your display name, avatar and account identifier may be visible to other users in the profile and messaging features.
Direct messages, blocks and safety reports
We process the text messages you send and the sender, recipient and conversation information needed to deliver them and display message history. We use your blocking choices to restrict unwanted contact. If you report a user or content, we process the report and relevant information you provide to investigate, respond and enforce our Terms of Service.
Breathing practice information
BIBO uses your selected practice settings to run guided breathing exercises and session information to display your practice history. The exercises are intended for general wellbeing.
Language preferences
We use your selected language to display the app in that language.
Connection information
Online requests include an IP address. We use the connection information needed to establish the connection and return responses for the features you request.
Support and privacy correspondence
If you contact us, we receive the contact details, message and attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond, verify requests, locate relevant records and resolve the issue.

We process information needed to provide requested features and perform our agreement with you. We also process information to meet legal obligations. Where applicable law permits, we rely on legitimate interests in maintaining security and preventing abuse. Where a use requires consent, we obtain that consent separately. We do not treat acceptance of the Terms of Service as consent to optional data uses.

You may decline optional information or permissions. If information is necessary for a feature, we explain the requirement; without it, that feature may not work.

2. Android device access

Internet access
BIBO uses an internet connection for sign-in, messaging, uploading your selected avatar and handling account requests.
Photos and media
When you choose an avatar, BIBO accesses the photo you select for upload. You may choose not to upload a photo. You can manage any media permission you have granted in Android settings.

Where Android requires permission, we request it before accessing the relevant data or device function. Declining an optional permission does not prevent use of unrelated features.

Release verification: Check this section against the release build and its manifest, including the photo picker and any network-status access. Additional permissions or background data access must be described before publication.

3. How personal data is shared

Other BIBO users
Recipients receive the messages you send and the profile information displayed with them. Other users may save or share information you choose to disclose, so consider what you send.
Service providers
Providers that support account authentication, SMS code delivery, hosting, data storage and email support process the information needed for those tasks on our behalf. An SMS delivery provider receives your mobile number and country calling code when you request a sign-in code. Our email service processes correspondence sent to our contact addresses. Providers acting on our behalf must follow applicable data protection requirements and our instructions.
Legal and safety disclosures
We may disclose information where required by law, in response to a valid legal request, or where necessary and legally permitted to protect a person's safety or establish, exercise or defend legal claims. We limit a disclosure to the relevant purpose.

If a new use or disclosure requires additional notice or consent, we provide that notice and obtain the required consent before it begins.

5. Retention and security

Account and profile data
We retain the account information and current profile needed to keep your account open and provide sign-in and profile features.
Messages and practice information
We retain service-held messages for delivery and conversation history, and any service-held practice information for the practice history or preferences you use. Deletion requests are handled as described in section 6.
Support, privacy and safety records
We retain relevant correspondence and report records for as long as needed to resolve the matter and meet any applicable legal requirement.

We delete personal data when it is no longer needed for its stated purpose. Where a specific law or legal preservation obligation requires retention, we restrict access and use to that purpose and delete the retained information when the obligation ends.

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including access restrictions and protection of data during transmission. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Where a personal data breach requires notification under applicable law, we notify the relevant authority and affected individuals as required.

6. Account and associated data deletion

In the app: Go to Me → Settings → Account Management → Delete Account, read the notice and select Delete Account to submit the request.

Outside the app: Email service@gfctextile.mom with the subject “Delete my BIBO account”. You can use this option without reinstalling or signing in to the app.

For an email request, provide your BIBO ID or registered phone number, including its country calling code, so that we can locate the account. We may ask for proportionate information to verify that the account belongs to you. Do not send your password or one-time verification codes by email. We will promptly tell you if more information is needed.

We will delete your account and associated personal data within 15 calendar days after an in-app request is submitted, or after an emailed request has been verified. A shorter mandatory legal deadline takes priority where applicable.

Deletion covers your sign-in and account records, phone number, profile details, avatar, service-held messages, breathing-practice records and preferences associated with the account. It also covers personal data held for us by service providers and in backups, subject only to the specific legal retention exceptions described in section 5.

Account deletion is permanent. You will lose access to the account and the records deleted with it. We cannot remove copies that another user has independently saved outside BIBO. Uninstalling the app or signing out does not delete your account. If you want deletion of particular data without closing your account, contact us and identify the data concerned; we will handle the request under applicable law.

See the BIBO Account Deletion Agreement for the full request process and consequences. A service restriction does not prevent you from using the email route to request deletion.

7. Your privacy rights and choices

You can ask what personal data we hold about you, request access or a readable copy, and request correction, completion, updating or deletion. Where applicable law provides the right, you may also object to processing or ask us to restrict it. If processing depends on your consent, you may withdraw that consent; this does not affect processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal.

Send a request to service@gfctextile.mom. We may reasonably verify your identity and will respond within the applicable legal period. If we cannot fulfil all or part of a request, we explain the reason unless the law prevents us from doing so.

For data access and correction requests governed by Hong Kong's Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance, the response period is generally 40 days, subject to the Ordinance's procedures and exceptions. Where Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law applies, we act on rights requests without delay and within 30 days; if the law permits an extension, we notify you of the reasons in advance and extend by no more than a further 30 days. The separate account-deletion commitment in section 6 remains applicable.

You may complain to the competent data protection authority, including Hong Kong's Privacy Commissioner for Personal Data or the Saudi Data & AI Authority where its law applies.

8. Age eligibility

BIBO is intended for users aged 18 or older, measured by the Gregorian calendar, who can legally enter into the Terms of Service. If you believe that a person under 18 has provided personal data through BIBO, contact us. We will investigate and take appropriate steps, including restricting the account and deleting data as required by law.

9. Changes to this Policy

We will publish updates on this page with an updated effective date. We give appropriate notice of material changes and, where required, obtain consent before processing personal data for a new purpose.
