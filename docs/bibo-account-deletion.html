This Account Deletion Agreement explains how to request permanent deletion of your BIBO account and what happens to your associated personal data. BIBO is operated by BELVEDERE HOLDING LIMITED, whose Google Play developer display name is Belvederey.

Account deletion is permanent. Read the consequences below before submitting a request. This Agreement should be read with the BIBO Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

1. How to request deletion

In the BIBO app

  1. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Me → Settings → Account Management → Delete Account.
  3. Read the deletion notice and select Delete Account to submit the request.

Without the app

Email service@gfctextile.mom with the subject “Delete my BIBO account”. Include your BIBO ID or registered phone number with country calling code and state that you want the account and associated personal data deleted.

Email a deletion request

The email option is available even if you have uninstalled the app, cannot sign in or your account is restricted. You do not need to reinstall BIBO to send the request. Selecting the email link opens your email app; send the message to submit your request.

2. Verifying account ownership

To protect your account, we may ask for proportionate information to verify that the request is yours. We use that information only to verify and handle the request, subject to the Privacy Policy. We promptly tell you if additional information is needed.

Never send your password or one-time verification codes by email. If you no longer have access to your registered phone number, explain this in your request so we can discuss an appropriate verification method.

3. When deletion is completed

We will delete your account and associated personal data within 15 calendar days:

  • For an in-app request, the period starts when you submit the request.
  • For an emailed request, the period starts once we have verified that the account belongs to you.

A shorter mandatory legal deadline applies where required. The deletion period includes account data held for us by service providers and in backups, except specific records that must be retained for legal reasons as explained in section 5.

4. Account data included in deletion

Deletion covers the personal data associated with your account that we hold, including:

  • Your phone number, BIBO ID, sign-in credentials and associated account records.
  • Your profile details, avatar and account-linked settings.
  • Your BIBO-held messages and associated conversation information.
  • Your breathing-practice settings and history held by BIBO.
  • Other personal data linked to your account, including copies processed for us by service providers, subject to section 5.

We instruct service providers holding the relevant data on our behalf to delete it as required. We cannot remove screenshots, downloaded copies or other information that another user has independently saved outside BIBO.

5. Specific records retained for legal reasons

We retain personal data after account deletion only where a specific legal requirement or legal preservation obligation requires it. Such records may include relevant support, rights-request or safety-report records where legally required.

We limit retained data to what the obligation requires, restrict access and use to that purpose, and delete it when the obligation ends. Where required and legally permitted, we explain the data retained, the reason and the applicable retention period or criteria. Retaining a specific legally required record does not keep your account active.

6. Consequences and important information

  • Once deletion is completed, you cannot sign in to or restore the deleted account.
  • You lose access to the profile, messages, practice history and other records deleted with the account.
  • Creating a new account later does not restore the deleted account or its data.
  • Signing out or uninstalling BIBO does not request account deletion.

By submitting a deletion request, you confirm that you are entitled to request deletion of the account and understand these consequences. This confirmation does not waive your privacy rights, mandatory consumer rights or other legal remedies.

If you want to delete particular personal data while keeping your account, email service@gfctextile.mom and describe your request. We handle it under applicable law.
